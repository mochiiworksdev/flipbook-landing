Heads up: Android will ask you something

Because Flipbook isn't on the Play Store, Android will show a warning that looks scary. It's normal for any app installed outside the Play Store.

  1. Tap Download APK above.
  2. If Chrome warns you, tap Download anyway.
  3. Open the file from Files → Downloads.
  4. When Android says "For your security…", tap Settings → allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  5. Tap Install. Done.
