What makes it different

📖

Real page flips

Open the cover, turn pages, watch them bend. Not a slideshow — an actual book with weight and shadow and sound.

✎

Type and draw

Bold, italic, highlight, colors, fonts. Pen, sign pen, highlighter, shapes, lasso. Everything you'd want in one place.

📌

Sticky wall

A corkboard for quick thoughts. Colorful sticky notes you can drag, pin, connect. Detective-board mode for brainstorming.

🔒

Yours, on your device

No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Everything lives in your browser's storage. Export the whole library as one file anytime.

See it in action

Dark mode for evenings and reading in bed.

Why this exists

Every note app I tried felt like a spreadsheet with a text box. I wanted something that felt like a real notebook — paper, ink, page turns, sticky notes. Not another cloud service that wants my email. So I made one.

It's free, it's offline, and it's yours. If it's useful to you, a tip is always appreciated but never expected.

Support the work

Flipbook is free, and it always will be. If it's useful to you and you'd like to say thanks, you can leave a tip. No subscriptions, no paid tiers, no pressure.
